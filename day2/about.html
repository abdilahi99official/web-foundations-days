About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple web app for capturing short ideas, tasks, and reminders. It is designed to help you record thoughts quickly before they disappear.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open QuickNotes in your browser.
  2. Type your note into the "New note" field.
  3. Click "Add Note" or press Ctrl + Enter to save it.

Features

  • Fast note capture
  • Simple, distraction-free interface
  • Keyboard-friendly workflow
